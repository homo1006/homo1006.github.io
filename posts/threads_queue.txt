=== 2 ===
Hot take: mAh is the most misleading number on a power bank box.
The number that decides if it charges your laptop is watts. Look for 65W USB-C PD.

=== 3 ===
Flying this winter? Power banks go in hand luggage only.
Airlines usually allow up to 100Wh without approval. A 20,000mAh bank is about 74Wh, so you're fine.

=== 4 ===
Quick maths for any power bank:
mAh × 3.7 ÷ 1000 = Wh
10,000mAh ≈ 37Wh
20,000mAh ≈ 74Wh
27,000mAh ≈ 100Wh (the airline limit)

=== 7 ===
How to find out what any appliance costs to run:
1. Plug it into an energy-monitoring smart plug
2. Read the kWh after a week
3. Multiply by the unit rate on your bill
That's it. No guessing.
